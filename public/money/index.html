Pocket Money

Ages 5+

Fill your Save, Spend and Share jars

A grown-up adds your pocket money. You choose which jar each coin goes in, and watch your Save jar grow towards something you want.

Start

How it works

  • Three jarsSave is for later, Spend is for soon, Share is for giving.
  • A goalPick what you're saving for and see how many coins are left to go.
  • Grown-upsTap Grown-ups to add coins or take them out when they're really spent. You'll need to sign in once.

Pocket Money is our own small app. The idea of giving every coin a job comes from envelope budgeting apps like Actual (MIT); none of its code is used here.

Coins are kept with your family's sign-in and are never shown to anyone else. Source: sauravsood/kids.sauravsood.com.
