Chess

Ages 6+

Learn the pieces, then play a gentle game

See how each piece moves by collecting pawns with it. When you're ready, play a slow game against the iPad. There's no clock and no score.

Start

How it works

  • LearnPick a piece and tap it. The dots show where it can go.
  • PlayYou are white. Tap a piece, then tap a dot. Take back a move any time.
  • Gentle, Easy, TrickyStart on Gentle. The iPad makes plenty of mistakes there.

The board is chessground from Lichess (GPL-3.0-or-later), with the cburnett pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett. The rules come from chess.js by Jeff Hlywa (BSD 2-Clause). The iPad's player is our own small one.

Because chessground is GPL, this chess app is GPL-3.0-or-later too. Its source is in sauravsood/kids.sauravsood.com.
