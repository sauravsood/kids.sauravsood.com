Pixel Art

Ages 6+

Make pictures one square at a time

Pick a colour and tap squares to fill them in. Add more frames and your picture moves. Stop whenever it looks right.

Start

Things to try

  • Pen and bucketThe pen fills one square. The bucket fills a whole patch.
  • FramesTap + under the frames to add another. Change it a little to make it move.
  • Keep itGrown-ups can save a picture to this iPad from the Export menu. Nothing goes online.
