What kind of animal?
Large oviraptorosaur dinosaur. Gigantoraptor belonged to the oviraptorosaurs. Its type fossil came from the Iren Dabasu Formation in Inner Mongolia, China. The scientific description estimated a length of about 8 metres and a mass of about 1,400 kilograms. The available evidence does not tell us exactly what it ate. This model’s large eyes, head ornament, colours and feather pattern are artistic ideas rather than features confirmed by fossils.

Research notes about Gigantoraptor

Research summary

Fossils of Gigantoraptor date to the Late Cretaceous and have been found in Inner Mongolia, China.

Learning from fossils

  • The known fossils do not support the model’s large eyes, head ornament, exact colours or precise feather pattern.
  • Diet remains unknown; it is not inferred from the oviraptorosaur name or from the model’s appearance.
  • About 8 metres is the paper’s whole-animal estimate and does not mean this model has been calibrated to exact fossil proportions.

A little museum made by a developer dad for his daughter

I’m Leon, a developer and a dad. When my daughter was three, dinosaur chases on television frightened her, so I made a 3D prehistoric animal museum where she could look quietly and listen only when she wanted to.

The museum is free, with no sign-up or adverts. Finding one interesting detail at a time is plenty.
