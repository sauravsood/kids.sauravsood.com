Music Lab

Ages 4+Six little music toys. Pick one and tap to play. Sound comes on when you touch it, so check the iPad isn't on silent.

  • Melody Maker

    Tap squares to write a tune, then press play.

  • Arpeggios

    Spin the colour wheel and hear the notes of each chord, one by one.

  • Chords

    Press a piano key and hear its chord. Flip between major and minor.

  • Piano Roll

    Watch famous tunes scroll by as coloured blocks, and play along.

  • Harmonics

    Touch the wobbly shapes to hear how one note hides inside another.

  • Strings

    Pluck long and short strings and hear which ones sound higher.
