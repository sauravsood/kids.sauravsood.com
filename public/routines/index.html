My Routines

Ages 3+

Tap the pictures as you do your day

Brush teeth, get dressed, read a book. Tap each picture when it's done. There are no scores and nothing to lose if you miss one.

Start

How it works

  • TodayBig picture cards. Tap one when you've done it.
  • This weekA calm look back at what you did. Missed days are just blank.
  • Grown-upsTap Grown-ups to pick the routines. You'll need to sign in once.

My Routines is our own small app, inspired by Loop Habit Tracker (GPL-3.0); none of its code is used here.

Routines are kept with your family's sign-in and are never shown to anyone else. Source: sauravsood/kids.sauravsood.com.
